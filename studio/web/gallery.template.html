KK Create · video editing inside Claude

KK Verse Studio: drop in a clip, type one line

Captions timed to your voice, effects that sit behind your head, and sound design. Pick anything below to watch it play. In Claude Code you would type the command shown under the preview.

/kk-captions kk-pop

Use it in Claude Code

Drag a video into the chat, then say what you want in plain words.

/kk-reel my-clip.mp4
/kk-captions truck-art, a bit higher
/kk-effects orbit from 3 to 6 seconds
/kk-effects big-word MYTH when I say "that is a myth"
/kk-sfx add a whoosh when I say "here's the thing"

Install once

Everything runs on your own computer. Your video is never uploaded.

git clone https://github.com/kkcreatemate/AIWo-Audience-Interest ~/.kkverse
bash ~/.kkverse/studio/install.sh

The speaker in the preview is a drawing. On your clip the studio finds the real person, so words and lights go between you and your wall. Sounds here are the same ones the studio mixes under your voice.
